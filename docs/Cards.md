### Cards

- Direct Shot. Simple Linear Function. Basic shot. Does not cause damage, need to be modified by another function.
- Parabola Arc. y = (t * k) ^ 2 - c.
- Sine Wave. y = sin(t) * A.
- Tangent Spike. y = tan(t).
- Archimedean Spiral. r = b * theta.
- Bernoulli`s Lemniscate. (x^2 + y^2)^2 = a^2(x^2 - y^2).
- Folium of Descartes. x^3 + y^3 = 3xy.
- Lissajous Curve. x = Asin(at + sigma), y = Bsin(bt).
- Vector. Draw a wall with fix length, which can destroy 1 bullet. (? Variations ?)
